BinCue Privacy

English

BinCue stores pickup item names, categories, places, notes, pickup cycles, reminder lead times, last set-out dates, and computed next pickup dates on your device.

BinCue does not require an account, does not include ads, and does not use analytics or third-party tracking SDKs in this release.

Notification permission is optional and is used only for local pickup reminders.

简体中文

此版本不需要账号，不包含广告，也不使用分析或第三方跟踪 SDK。

通知权限是可选的，仅用于本地收运提醒。
